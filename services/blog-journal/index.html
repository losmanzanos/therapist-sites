Services

A blog you can actually keep updated.

Password-protected admin access to write, edit, and publish posts yourself, styled to match your site automatically, and structured for search from the first post.

A lot of practice sites launch with a blog, then never post again because updating it means emailing a developer. This is built the other way: you get a simple, password-protected login, write a post, hit publish. No code, no separate CMS to learn, no call to me unless you want one.

It's also not a bolt-on afterthought visually, posts inherit your site's fonts, colors, and layout automatically, so a new post looks like it's always been part of the site, not like it was pasted in from somewhere else.

  • Password-protected admin login: write, edit, and delete your own posts without touching code.
  • Matches your site automatically: new posts inherit your fonts, colors, and layout, no separate design system to maintain.
  • Built for search from the first post: each post gets its own meta title, description, and BlogPosting schema.
  • A real content strategy, if you want it: ongoing copy & monthly posts are available as a standing retainer at $150/hr.

What it costs

The Blog / Journal add-on is $850 on top of either the Solo or Group package.
