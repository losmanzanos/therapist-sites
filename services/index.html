Services

Two doors. Pick the one that matches your situation.

Either you need a website, or you have one and it isn't doing its job. Everything I sell falls under one of those, and the price for each is on the sheet.

Door one

You need a website.

Starting from nothing, or from a site you're ready to leave behind. I build it from a blank file, write the first draft of every page with you, wire it for search, and hand you the keys: domain, hosting and code, all in your name.

  1. Solo practice website$2,800

    Homepage, about, a page per specialty, FAQ, policies, contact. The base build, and most of what a solo practice ever needs.

  2. Group practice website$4,200

    The solo build plus a page for each clinician, their own schema, and a directory that routes people to the right one. Covers you and four more; bigger groups add a page per clinician.

  3. Blog, added to either+$850

    A login where you publish articles yourself. Only worth it if you'll actually write; I'll say so if I think you won't.

SEO is not a line item. Every build ships with structured data, a sitemap, llms.txt and per-page meta written for real searches. What that covers. Google Business Profile setup is included too.

HIPAA, briefly. The site itself holds no client records, so the contact form is the part that matters. On the first call you pick between a short form that asks people not to share clinical details, and one routed through a service that signs a Business Associate Agreement, like your EHR's own contact widget. How to decide.

Not a therapist? The build is the same and so is the price. Write to me here, or start at Studio Null, the rest of the shop: websites for anyone, web applications, and the odd piece of hardware.

Door two

You have a website, and it isn't working.

On Squarespace, Wix or WordPress and not ready to start over? Fine. I'll work inside what you have. Sometimes the right answer is a rebuild and I'll tell you if it is, with the number next to it, so you can compare.

  1. A look, firstFree

    Send the link. I check what Google and the AI engines actually see (title tags, schema, speed, whether the site is even indexed) and where your contact form sends what people type into it. You get a short, plain note on what's wrong and what it would cost to fix. No obligation.

  2. Fixes, design and copy inside your platform$150 / hr, or a fixed quote

    Faster pages, a cleaner layout, copy that sounds like you, SEO basics that were never set up. Sanare started this way.

  3. Google Business Profile, for a site I didn't buildfrom $200

    Claimed, filled in properly, linked back correctly. The single most-skipped thing on a therapist's web presence.

  4. Move it off the platformfrom $250 on top of a build

    When you do decide to leave: redirects, sitemap, schema and your search history all carry over, so Google doesn't forget you exist for three months.

Already know it's a rebuild? That's door one. The migration line above is the only thing you'd add.

Not sure which door?
