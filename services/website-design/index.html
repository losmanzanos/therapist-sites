Services

A website built around your practice, not a template.

Every site starts from a blank file, not a theme. Homepage, about, a dedicated page per service or specialty, FAQ, privacy policy, terms & conditions, and contact, hand-coded to match how you actually practice.

Most "custom" therapist websites are a page builder with your logo dropped in. Same layout, same sections, same stock photography as the practice three towns over. That's not a knock on those tools, it's just not what I build.

Every site I ship is written from scratch in plain HTML and CSS, no WordPress core to patch, no drag-and-drop builder shipping code you didn't ask for. That means it loads fast, it's genuinely yours, and it looks like nothing else in your specialty's search results.

  • Full page set: homepage, about, services overview, FAQ, privacy policy, terms & conditions, and a contact form wired straight to your inbox.
  • A page for every service or specialty: not one generic services tab, a dedicated page per modality, each targeted to that specialty and your service area.
  • SEO-structured from day one: unique meta titles & descriptions, Open Graph tags, and schema.org markup so Google understands exactly what your practice offers.
  • Mobile-first & fast: responsive layout and compressed images built to hold up under Google's Core Web Vitals, tested down to phone width.
  • Google Business Profile setup: claimed, optimized, and linked back to your new site correctly, included at no extra cost.
  • True digital ownership: your domain, hosting, and code live under your name. If we ever part ways, your site doesn't disappear.

What it costs

Solo practice builds start at $2,800. Group practices with up to four additional clinicians, $4,200. Both include the full page set above and Google Business Profile setup.
