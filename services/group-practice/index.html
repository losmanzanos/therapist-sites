Services

One site. Every clinician. No one lost in a directory.

Up to four additional clinician profiles, each with their own schema markup, plus a directory built to route the right client to the right person, not a faceless grid of headshots.

A group practice site has a harder job than a solo one: it has to hold every clinician's individual story and specialty without collapsing into a generic staff-directory page. That's the whole design problem this package solves.

This started as the build for my wife's practice, Full Bloom Counseling Group, seven clinicians, one site, each with their own page and their own voice. It's now the template every group build starts from.

  • Everything in Solo practice — Homepage, about, services, FAQ, privacy policy, terms & conditions, and contact, all included.
  • Up to 4 additional clinician profiles — Each with their own bio, specialties, and photo, not a shared template.
  • Individual schema markup per clinician — Per-clinician Person schema so each therapist can be found individually in search.
  • A directory that actually routes clients — Built to guide the right client to the right clinician, not just list names.

What it costs

Group practice builds are $2,500, covering the base build plus up to four additional clinicians.
