Services

Get found by the client who's already looking for you.

Every practice I build is structured for search from the first commit, meta tags, schema markup, and the newer stuff too: sitemaps built for AI answer engines, not just Google.

SEO for a therapist site isn't keyword stuffing, it's making sure the technical basics are actually done: a real meta title and description on every page, schema.org markup that tells Google (and now ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Mode) exactly who you are and what you treat, and a site that loads fast enough that people don't bounce before it renders.

This isn't an add-on I bolt on later. It's built into every site from the first page. The Google Business Profile setup below is the one piece that lives outside your website itself, and it matters just as much.

  • Schema.org structured data — LocalBusiness, FAQPage, and per-clinician Person schema on Group builds, so search engines can read your practice, not just guess at it.
  • Sitemap, robots.txt & llms.txt — So Google, Bing, and AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity can all find and correctly describe your practice.
  • Unique meta titles & descriptions — Every page written to answer a real search, not a generic template line repeated across the site.
  • Google Business Profile setup — Claimed, optimized, and linked back to your site correctly, available as an add-on from $200.

How it's priced

On-page SEO is included in every Solo and Group build at no extra cost. Google Business Profile setup and SEO/authority carryover for migrations are priced as add-ons.
