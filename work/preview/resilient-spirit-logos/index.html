Four logo directions

Each one is shown on oyster, on marsh green, and at the small sizes a logo has to survive: favicon, social avatar, the corner of a phone screen. Colors pull from the site. Pick one to develop, or mix pieces of two.

A. Spartina

Marsh grass that bends in the wind and stands back up. It's the plant that holds the Lowcountry coast together. One blade carries a gold seed head.

B. Growth rings

A tree records every year in its rings, the hard ones included. The gold ring is the hard year. The rings after it grow a little thicker where it pinched, filling the mark back in.
