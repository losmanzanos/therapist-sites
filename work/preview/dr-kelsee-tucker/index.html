Couples therapy

Three methods with real evidence behind them, chosen for your pattern, not mine.

Every couple that walks in has a pattern. One pursues, one withdraws. One keeps score, one goes quiet. By the second session I can usually name it, and naming it is the first thing that changes. From there, the method depends on you.

Emotionally Focused Therapy for couples whose fights are really about whether the other person will be there. The Gottman Method for couples who need to learn to argue without damage. Integrative Behavioral Couples Therapy for couples who've been trying to change each other for a decade and are ready to try acceptance instead.

About Dr. Tucker

A psychologist, not a generalist.

I am a licensed psychologist with a PhD, and I have spent my career on relationships: couples, families, and the individuals inside them. I practiced in Columbus, Ohio, before moving the practice to Indiana, and many of my Ohio couples came with me by video.

I don't take insurance, on purpose. It keeps your records yours and keeps our work free of someone else's session limits. I'll give you a superbill after every session, and most PPO plans reimburse about half.
