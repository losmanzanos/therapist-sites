Healing starts here

Hi, I'm Dr. Tucker. I work with couples, almost exclusively.

I'm a licensed psychologist and relationship specialist. Every couple that walks in has a pattern. One pursues, one withdraws. One keeps score, one goes quiet. By the second session I can usually name it, and naming it is the first thing that changes.

From there, the method depends on you: Emotionally Focused Therapy, the Gottman Method, or Integrative Behavioral Couples Therapy. All three have real evidence behind them. I'll explain the choice in plain language.

I practiced in Columbus, Ohio, before moving the practice to Indiana, and many of my Ohio couples came with me by video. Under PSYPACT I can see couples in 40+ states, and I do.

I don't take insurance, on purpose. It keeps your records yours and our work free of someone else's session limits. You get a superbill after every session, and most PPO plans reimburse about half.
