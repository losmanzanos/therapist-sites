Working with Colorado couples

What's different about doing this from Colorado.

Boulder and Denver have strong therapy communities, but private-pay couples specialists with doctoral training are a smaller group, and the ones in Boulder are often booked months out.

Colorado was in the first wave of PSYPACT states, effective July 2020. If you split time between Colorado and a non-member state, we'll plan sessions around where you'll be.

Insurance and reimbursement in Colorado

I'm a private-pay practice. Colorado PPO plans reimburse out-of-network psychology at varying rates. The Colorado Division of Insurance requires plans to tell you the out-of-network rate on request. FSA and HSA cards work for the full fee.

The methods

Emotionally Focused Therapy, the Gottman Method and Integrative Behavioral Couples Therapy. Which one we use depends on your pattern, and I'll explain the choice in plain language in the first two sessions. More on how I work with couples.

The one rule

You both have to be in Colorado, or another PSYPACT state, when the session starts.
