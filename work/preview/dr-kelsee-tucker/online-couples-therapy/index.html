Online couples therapy

Couples therapy by video, with a psychologist, in 40+ states.

Under PSYPACT, a psychologist licensed in one member state can see clients by video in every other member state. I'm licensed in Indiana and hold the compact's APIT/E.Passport credential (Mobility #16033), which is what makes this legal and verifiable. The one rule: you and your partner both have to be physically in a participating state at the time of the session.

How a video session works

We meet on a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. You can be in the same room or in two different places, as long as both places are in a member state. Sessions are 55 or 90 minutes, same as in person, and the fee is the same: $350 and $525. Superbills go out after each session for out-of-network reimbursement.

When in person makes more sense

Some couples do better in a room, and intensives are almost always in person. My office is at Elevate Offices in Brownsburg, Indiana, twenty minutes west of downtown Indianapolis. Couples travel in for half-day and full-day intensives from across the Midwest.
