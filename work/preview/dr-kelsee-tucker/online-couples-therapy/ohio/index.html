Working with Ohio couples

What's different about doing this from Ohio.

Columbus, Cincinnati and Cleveland each have a handful of couples specialists trained in EFT and the Gottman Method, and they fill up fast. Video with someone an hour west, on the same time zone, is often the quicker route.

Ohio joined PSYPACT in July 2021. Nothing about our work changes because I'm across the state line; the compact handles the licensing side.

Insurance and reimbursement in Ohio

I'm a private-pay practice. Ohio plans from Anthem, Medical Mutual and Aetna PPO products typically have out-of-network behavioral health benefits. Superbills go out the same day as your session. FSA and HSA cards work for the full fee.

The methods

Emotionally Focused Therapy, the Gottman Method and Integrative Behavioral Couples Therapy. Which one we use depends on your pattern, and I'll explain the choice in plain language in the first two sessions. More on how I work with couples.

The one rule

You both have to be in Ohio, or another PSYPACT state, when the session starts.
