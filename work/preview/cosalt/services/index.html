Services

Service lines

Fractional and interim roles

We staff the gap while you recruit: a revenue manager, an auditor, a finance director, or a few days a week of someone who already knows the work.

Compliance review and deficiency identification

Returns and license filings checked against what the code requires. Each deficiency gets documented.

Taxpayer communication and education

Clear notices and guidance for the businesses you collect from. Fewer surprises usually means fewer disputes.

Audit training, support and sampling

Training manuals and statistical sampling templates your staff keeps using, plus hands-on audit help when the team is stretched.

Data analysis and risk assessment

Analytics on your collection and license data to flag anomalies and rank where an audit is worth the hours.

Program design and process improvement

Procedures written down and ordinances brought current, so the program doesn’t live in one person’s head.

SUTS, and the systems around it

Our team has worked across several tax collection platforms and knows the Colorado Sales and Use Tax System (SUTS) well.

That matters most in two situations: when a jurisdiction is behind on compliance, and when it has just lost the employee who understood how everything was set up.

How we approach the numbers

We start with the records. Our analysts use data analytics and statistical sampling to find anomalies and measure risk, so the decisions that follow rest on evidence your finance director can defend.

Accuracy comes first. Communication comes a close second.
