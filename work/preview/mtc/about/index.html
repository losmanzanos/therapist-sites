How MTC started

A psychologist who believes in groups wanted one for herself and her colleagues. So she built it.

Jennifer Joseph, PsyD, CGP, PMH-C
Licensed psychologist in New York and New Jersey
drjenjoseph.com

Meet Dr. J

Dr. Jennifer Joseph (aka Dr. J) is a Licensed Psychologist, Certified Group Psychotherapist, Perinatal Mental Health Certified Psychologist, and the Founder of Montclair Therapy Collective. Dr. J has more than 20 years of experience providing individual, couples, and group therapy to adults and families with a wide range of problems and challenges.

Dr. J’s belief in the power of authentic connection extends beyond her work with her patients. Since founding MTC in 2022, Dr. J has helped create opportunities for therapists and allied professionals throughout the Montclair area to meet one another, build professional relationships, exchange referrals, and continue learning together. As an experienced group facilitator, Dr. J identified a need for herself and other mental health providers to have a consistent group where they can genuinely connect in Montclair and the surrounding area.

Dr. J’s commitment to group therapy has helped shape an important part of the vision for Montclair Therapy Collective: making specialized groups and additional sources of support easier for both clients and therapists to find.

For Dr. J, Montclair Therapy Collective is an extension of something she has already seen firsthand:

When providers are better connected and community members are more easily able to find the resources available to them, everyone has a better chance of finding health care that truly fits.

Where MTC is headed

Since 2022A regular gathering

What began as a regular in-person gathering of mental health professionals has now grown into a large and well connected community, both online and in person, where local clinicians can find one another, learn from one another, make meaningful referrals, and share resources that strengthen mental health care in the greater Montclair community.

SoonA local directory

Montclair Therapy Collective is expanding its vision to include a localized directory of providers that aims to bring together community members, therapists, and all local mental health professionals in one place, making it easier to find the people, services, information, and resources they are looking for.

SoonContinuing Education

Continuing Education credits will be available, provided by local community members, making our learning experience more meaningful and worthwhile.
