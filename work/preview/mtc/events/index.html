How it works

  1. Save a seat on Luma

    Each event has its own page. Register there and Luma sends you a reminder before it starts.

  2. Give what you can

    Networking nights have a $10 suggested donation. Workshops are usually $15. Rooms are small, so seats do run out.

  3. Show up as you are

    Most people come alone. Jen makes introductions, and you’ll leave knowing a few more colleagues.
