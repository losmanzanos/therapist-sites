A local group for psychotherapists and allied professionals. We meet for networking nights, workshops with CE and small groups, all close to home.

One or two emails a month, from Jen.

Private practice can be a lonely job. Most of the week it’s you, a client and a closed door. MTC gets local clinicians together a few times a season, so the people you refer to are people you’ve actually met.

Founded by

Jen Joseph

PsyD, CGP, PMH-C. Licensed psychologist in Montclair.

Jen has been a psychologist for more than twenty years. She sees individuals, couples and groups at her office at 96 Park Street, in person and online, with a lot of her work centered on chronic pain, the perinatal years and relationships.

Groups run through almost everything she does. She’s been a Certified Group Psychotherapist since 2011, finished the program at the Center for Group Studies in New York in 2020, and leads supervision and consultation groups where therapists bring their hardest cases and work through them together.

MTC takes that same idea out of the office. Clinicians do better work when they aren’t doing it alone.
