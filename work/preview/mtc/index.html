Private practice can be a lonely job.

Most of the week it’s you, a client and a closed door. MTC gets local clinicians into the same room a few times a season, so you have colleagues to call when a case gets hard, and names you trust when a client needs a referral.

Therapists, psychologists, social workers, counselors, grad students and associates are all welcome, along with the dietitians, coaches and bodyworkers our clients see too.

Come on your own. Most people do.

Meet Dr. JFounder, Montclair Therapy Collective

Jennifer Joseph, PsyD, CGP, PMH-C. Licensed psychologist in New York and New Jersey.

Dr. Jennifer Joseph (aka Dr. J) is a Licensed Psychologist, Certified Group Psychotherapist, Perinatal Mental Health Certified Psychologist, and the Founder of Montclair Therapy Collective. Dr. J has more than 20 years of experience providing individual, couples, and group therapy to adults and families with a wide range of problems and challenges.

Since founding MTC in 2022, Dr. J has helped create opportunities for therapists and allied professionals throughout the Montclair area to meet one another, build professional relationships, exchange referrals, and continue learning together.

When providers are better connected and community members are more easily able to find the resources available to them, everyone has a better chance of finding health care that truly fits.

Frequently asked questions

Who can come to MTC events?

Psychotherapists and allied professionals in and around Montclair. That includes licensed therapists, psychologists, social workers and counselors, grad students and associates, and professionals like dietitians, coaches and bodyworkers who work alongside therapists.

Do I need to be a member?

No. Events are open to any clinician in the area, and you register for each one on Luma. A paid membership is planned alongside the directory.

What does it cost?

It depends on the event. Networking nights have a $10 suggested donation and workshops usually a $15 suggested donation. Each event’s Luma page has the details.

Do the workshops offer CE credit?

Continuing Education credits are on the way. When a workshop carries CE, its event listing will say so.

I don’t know anyone. Should I still come?

Yes. Most people come on their own, and the events are set up for meeting new people.

When will the directory open?

Once enough members are ready to be listed. Tell us about your practice and you’ll hear first.

Can I lead a workshop or host a gathering?

Jen would love to hear your idea. Send a short proposal.
