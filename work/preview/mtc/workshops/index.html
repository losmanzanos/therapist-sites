Workshops

Ninety minutes of clinical learning, taught by a clinician who practices down the street from you.

Small room, specific topics

These aren’t conference lectures. A presenter covers one subject they know well, with learning objectives and a case example, and there’s time to ask the question you actually have.

Because the presenter is local, the conversation keeps going. You can refer to them, consult with them, or see them at the next networking night.

Continuing Education credits are on the way. When a workshop carries CE, its event listing will say so.

Topics so far

  • Recognizing mind-body and neuroplastic symptoms in clinical practice
  • Taboo and “Pure O” OCD, and an introduction to ERP
  • Somatic Experiencing® for complex trauma
  • Adult ADHD, with a focus on women
