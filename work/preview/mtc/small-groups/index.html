Ways to connect

Small groups

The same few faces, meeting more than once. Book nights, consultation groups and peer groups for clinicians who want more than a name tag.

Tell Jen what you’re looking for
  • Book nights

    A handful of clinicians read the same book and talk it over in someone’s living room. It’s the easiest way into a small group.

  • Consultation groups

    Bring the case that’s keeping you up. A steady group of colleagues helps you think it through, and you do the same for them.

  • Peer groups

    Clinicians at the same stage or with the same specialty, meeting regularly to compare notes on the work and the business of it.

Led by someone who does this for a living

Dr. J is a Certified Group Psychotherapist with more than 20 years of experience providing individual, couples and group therapy. Groups are the heart of how she works, and the reason MTC exists in the first place.

She saw a need, for herself and for other mental health providers, to have a consistent group where they can genuinely connect in Montclair and the surrounding area.

If you want in on a small group, or want to start one, tell her what you have in mind.
