Owner and clinical director

Sarah Freeze

Curio is my practice. For years it went by my name.

I renamed it when it stopped being only me. I specialize in EMDR and in adults who were abused or neglected as children, and I supervise the clinical work here.

My weekly practice is full right now. I still offer EMDR intensives, and I brought on Lora Hope so the door could stay open.

Lora Hope

Lora works with women whose lives look put-together from the outside and feel like too much from the inside.

Many of them find her in the middle of a change: a divorce, a new career, menopause, the last kid leaving home. The work is closing the distance between “everything’s fine” and what is really going on underneath.
