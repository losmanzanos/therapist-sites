Website privacy policy

Written in plain language. If anything here is unclear, ask us.

Contents

Questions: info@resilientspiritsc.org

This policy covers this website, resilientspiritsc.org. Your health information as a client is covered separately by our Notice of Privacy Practices, which you receive at intake and which follows HIPAA.

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect what you type into it: your name, email address, phone number, who you are reaching out about, the state you live in, and any message you include. We use it only to respond to you and set up a consultation.

The site does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies or social media pixels. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use those logs to identify you.

How the form works

Your message travels over an encrypted connection to our web host, which passes it to an email delivery service that sends it to the practice inbox and sends you a confirmation. These providers handle the message only to deliver it.

Email is not a perfectly secure channel. Please keep detailed health information out of the contact form and save it for your consult call or the secure client portal.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing. We will not add you to a mailing list.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete a contact form message that has not become part of a client record. Email info@resilientspiritsc.org or call or text (843) 212-6965.

Children

This site is meant for adults, including parents and guardians reaching out for a child. If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date below.
