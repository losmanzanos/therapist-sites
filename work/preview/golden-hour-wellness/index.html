Welcome

A practice built around the person in the room.

Golden Hour Wellness Colorado was founded by Shayla Martinez-O’Brien, a Licensed Professional Counselor with 11 years of clinical practice. What began as one clinician is now a small, hand-picked team.

We believe true healing comes from an increased ability to tolerate challenging emotion, paired with a clearer understanding of your own value system. That work is slow, human, and worth it, and you don’t have to do it alone.

You can expect a co-pilot on your journey. Shayla Martinez-O’Brien, LPC · Founder

How we help

Specialized care, without the clinical chill.

Every therapist here brings a different lane of expertise. We’ll help you find the right fit on your first call, even if that fit turns out to be somewhere else.

01

Trauma & EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing alongside other trauma-focused approaches, in person or via telehealth.
02

Anxiety & Depression

Solution-oriented, CBT-informed work on how mood, thoughts and behavior shape one another, plus tools you can actually use.
03

Substance Use & Recovery

Licensed addiction counseling for people living with substance use disorders, or anyone feeling stuck and disconnected.
04

Couples & Relationships

Family stress, work stress, and the big pivots. Getting through change with more clarity and a stronger sense of purpose.
05

Life Transitions

A move, a new job, a new baby, an illness or a separation. Individual sessions for whatever the change shakes loose.
06

Mama’s Golden Hour

A six-week virtual group for Colorado mothers. Fertility, pregnancy, postpartum and every stage of parenting.
