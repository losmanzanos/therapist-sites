Trauma-informed · Denver & telehealth across Colorado

Three doors. Pick the one you're standing at.

Therapy for yourself or your relationship. Consultation when it's someone you love who is struggling. Training when it's your whole team. All trauma-informed, all paced to fit real life.

Here's a plain look at the four ways to work with me: who each one is built for, what it costs, and how to know where to start.

i.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one, trauma-informed therapy for adults carrying anxiety, depression, burnout, trauma, or a season of life that's harder than it should be. Sessions are paced to what your nervous system can hold. No worksheets, no script, just collaborative work toward feeling more like yourself.

$175 per session·50 minutes·sliding scale available

Explore individual therapy
ii.

Couples Therapy

For partners at any stage, newly struggling or years into the same argument, who want to understand each other again. We slow down the pattern, name what's underneath it, and build communication that holds up outside the therapy room.

$200 per session·60 minutes·partners welcome at any stage

Explore couples therapy
iii.

Family Consultation

For the people around someone in a psychiatric hospital, a treatment program, a jail, or a long stretch of untreated illness. Not necessarily therapy: a scheduled conversation with someone who has worked inside those systems and can tell you what the diagnosis means, what the options are, how to help without enabling, and what to do this week. Family therapy is available if you want it.

Scheduled sessions·Denver or video·one conversation or ongoing

Explore family consultation
iv.

Consulting & Training

Trauma-informed practice, behavioral health program development, staff resilience, compassion fatigue, and how an organization responds when something hard happens to its people. For schools, nonprofits, treatment programs, and helping professionals. Workshops and groups for families and professionals are part of this too.

By inquiry·in-person in Denver or virtual·single trainings or ongoing engagements

Explore consulting & training

Common Questions

Sorting out where to start.

If you're working on something for yourself, start with individual therapy. If the difficulty lives between you and a partner, start with couples therapy. If the person struggling is someone you love and you need to understand what's happening and what to do, that's family consultation. If you're responsible for a team carrying hard work, consulting and training is the right door. A free 15-minute call sorts it out quickly.

I'm an out-of-network provider and don't bill insurance directly. I provide monthly superbills you can submit to your plan for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and a limited number of sliding-scale slots are available.

Therapy is delivered by secure telehealth to anyone located in Colorado. Family consultations are in person in Denver or by video. Consulting and training is available in person around Denver and the Front Range, or virtually for organizations anywhere in the country.

Not sure which fits?

That's what the free 15-minute call is for: a short conversation to figure out the right starting point together.
