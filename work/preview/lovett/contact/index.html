What Happens Next

After you send a note.

1. I read it myself

Every inquiry comes straight to me, not to an assistant or an intake team. You'll hear back within two business days, usually sooner.

2. We set up a free 15-minute call

A short, no-obligation conversation about what's bringing you in, what you've already tried, and whether I'm the right person for it. You're welcome to ask me anything.

3. If it's a fit, we schedule

I'll send intake paperwork and a secure video link, and we start from there. If it isn't a fit, I'll point you toward someone who is. That happens sometimes, and it's a good outcome.

You don't have to have it all figured out. You just have to be willing to begin.
