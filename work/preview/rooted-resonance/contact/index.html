Before you send it

What should I put in the message?

A sentence or two is plenty. Keep it general. Email is not a secure channel, so leave out symptoms, diagnoses and anything private, and we will find a better place for the rest.

When will I hear back?

Usually within two business days. If you have not heard from me by then, call 513-497-8595.

Does the first conversation cost anything?

No. If it looks like a fit, we set up a short conversation at no cost. If it does not, I will say so and try to point you somewhere useful.
