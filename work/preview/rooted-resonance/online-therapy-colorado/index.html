Front Range to Western Slope

I see clients by video anywhere in Colorado. Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, Pueblo, and mountain towns where the one therapist in the valley might be your neighbor, your kid’s coach, or both.

A private room, a parked car, a closed office at lunch. Whatever gives you fifty uninterrupted minutes.

What brings people here

Usually a pattern they understand completely and cannot stop. Saying yes when they mean no. Holding it together at work and falling apart at home. People pleasing that used to work and now just costs.

Somatic therapy works with what happens in your body before you decide anything. Relational therapy takes the new answer into your actual relationships. Queer, kink, poly and neurodivergent clients will not have to explain themselves first.

Insurance in Colorado

I am in network with Anthem, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Optum and others, some through Headway or Rula. Plans vary, so the full list is on the FAQ and I can check yours. Self pay is $150, and you can ask for a Good Faith Estimate.

If you need help now

Therapy is not crisis care. Call or text 988 any time, or reach Colorado Crisis Services at 1-844-493-8255, or text TALK to 38255.

Colorado questions

Can we meet in person?

Therapy is by video. Sound healing is currently offered in person, so ask me about the location and the next open sessions.

Do you take Anthem in Colorado?

Yes, I am in network with Anthem. Send me your plan and I will confirm the cost before we start.

I live in a small town. Is video really private enough?

Often more private than a waiting room where you might run into someone you know. You just need a spot where you will not be overheard.

How do I start?

A sentence or two through the contact form is plenty. I reply, usually within two business days, and the first conversation costs nothing.
