Online therapy
in Ohio

Somatic and relational therapy by video, for anyone in the state.

From Cleveland to the river towns

I see clients by video anywhere in Ohio. Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Dayton, Toledo, Akron, and the long stretches of the state between them, where the nearest therapist who works with the body is often an hour each way.

What you need is a room with a door that closes and a connection that holds for fifty minutes. A parked car works too. People use it more than you would think.

What we work on

The same thing everyone here works on, in one form or another: the adaptations that kept you safe and are now costing you. People pleasing. Masking. Staying busy so nothing can catch up with you.

Somatic therapy works with what your body does before you decide anything. Relational therapy takes it into your relationships, whatever shape they are. If you are queer, kinky, non monogamous or neurodivergent, none of that needs explaining first.

Insurance in Ohio

I am in network with Medical Mutual of Ohio and Anthem, along with Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Optum, UMR and Carelon, some of them through Headway or Rula. Coverage varies by plan, so the full list is on the FAQ and I am glad to check yours. Paying yourself? Sessions are $150, and you can ask for a Good Faith Estimate.

Time and crisis support

Ohio is on Eastern time. When we book, I confirm the time in yours.

Therapy is not crisis care. In a crisis, call or text 988 at any hour. The Ohio CareLine, 1-800-720-9616, is a free support line that also answers around the clock.

Ohio questions

Do you have an office in Ohio?

No. Therapy is by video only, which is why it works the same in Toledo as in Cincinnati.

Do you take Medical Mutual of Ohio?

Yes, I am in network with Medical Mutual of Ohio. Plans differ, so send me yours and I will confirm what a session would cost you.

Can I start if I am moving to or from Ohio?

Therapy follows where you are physically sitting during the session. If you will be living in Ohio, Arizona or Colorado, we can work together. Tell me your timeline and we will sort it out.

How do I start?

A sentence or two through the contact form is enough. I reply, usually within two business days, and the first conversation costs nothing.
