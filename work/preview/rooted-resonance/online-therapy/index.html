Body-based work, by video

Somatic therapy by video can sound like a contradiction. Most of it is noticing: where a sentence lands in your chest, what your breath does when a hard subject comes up, the reflex to brace. None of that needs us in the same room.

We talk, but not the whole time. Some sessions are conversational. Some are quiet. A room where you will not be interrupted for the hour helps more than any particular setup.

Two kinds of therapy, both online

  • Somatic therapy for people who can explain every pattern and still find themselves repeating it.
  • Relational therapy for relationships of any shape or number, and for people between them.

Both are psychotherapy, provided under my Licensed Professional Counselor credentials, and both are queer, kink and poly affirming.

Not in one of the three states

Then therapy is not something I can offer you, and I would rather say that up front. Breathwork can be done remotely from anywhere, and workshops are open to anyone. Neither is psychotherapy.

A few practical answers

Do I need to live in Ohio, Arizona or Colorado?

For therapy, yes. Counseling licenses are state by state, so therapy is limited to the states where I am licensed. Sound healing, breathwork and workshops are open to anyone.

Is sound healing online too?

No. Sound healing is currently offered in person. Breathwork can be done remotely.

Do I need a diagnosis or a referral?

No. You can reach out directly. Send a short note through the contact form or call 513-497-8595.
