Scope of Practice

Two kinds of work, clearly separated.

Therapy

Somatic and relational therapy is psychotherapy. I provide it as a Licensed Professional Counselor to residents of Ohio, Arizona and Colorado. It can include assessment, treatment planning and clinical care.

Coaching & Wellness

Sound healing, breathwork and workshops are not psychotherapy. They are educational and experiential practices supporting rest, body awareness and personal growth, and they are open to anyone, anywhere.

Coaching and wellness services are not a substitute for mental health treatment, diagnosis or crisis care, and no therapeutic relationship is created by booking them. If you are unsure which one fits, start with a conversation. Sorting that out together is part of the work. In an emergency, call or text 988.

Choosing where to begin

Which offering should I start with?

If you are not sure, start with a conversation. It costs nothing, and working out which door fits is part of what we do in it.

If you already know that talking has stopped helping, sound healing or breathwork may be the quicker way in.

I am already in therapy with someone else. Can I book sound healing or breathwork?

Yes. A regulation practice alongside therapy you already have is one of the reasons people book sound healing. Both are wellness services, so they sit next to that work rather than replacing it.

Why is therapy limited to three states when everything else is nationwide?

Counseling licenses are state by state. I am licensed in Ohio, Arizona and Colorado, so therapy stops at those state lines. Sound healing, breathwork and workshops are not psychotherapy, which is why anyone can book them. More on online therapy.

Does insurance cover all of this?

Only the therapy. I am in network with Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, United Healthcare and several other plans, some through Headway or Rula, and the full list is on the FAQ. Sound healing, breathwork and workshops are not billable to insurance.
