Who sits in the other chair matters

Plenty of queer, kinky and non monogamous people have sat across from a therapist who meant well and still needed it all explained. You mention a second partner, or a play party, or your pronouns, and the next three sessions are about that instead of what you came in with.

I am a queer, ethically non monogamous, kink and sex positive Licensed Professional Counselor, and my private practice has served LGBTQIA+, neurodivergent and kinky communities since 2020. Your structure and your practices are context. They become the presenting problem only if you say so.

You can skip the glossary.

What we work on

Mostly the same things anyone brings to therapy. The difference is that the parts which usually need translating are already understood.

  • Losing yourself inside a relationship and only noticing afterward.
  • Jealousy, comparison, and the fear of being replaceable.
  • Opening a relationship, closing one, or renegotiating the terms.
  • Years of shaping yourself to fit your family, your partners or your culture, and the exhaustion that comes after.
  • Repair after a rupture, including the kind nobody names out loud.
  • Performing for belonging, and finding out who you are when you stop.

Some of that happens in somatic therapy, where we work with the adaptations that once protected you. Some of it is relational therapy, for partnerships of any shape or number and for people working through them on their own. Most people move between the two.

Where this is available

Therapy is by telehealth for residents of Ohio, Arizona and Colorado, the three states where I am licensed. Sessions are $150 self pay, and I am in network with several plans, including Aetna, Cigna and United Healthcare, some through Headway or Rula. The full list is on the FAQ.

Outside those states, therapy is off the table. Sound healing, breathwork and workshops are wellness services rather than psychotherapy, so they are open to anyone.

Two more things people ask

Do you work with neurodivergent clients?

Yes. LGBTQIA+, neurodivergent and kinky communities are who my private practice has served for six years. If you have spent years masking, that is exactly the kind of adaptation this work is built around. More on neurodivergent affirming therapy.

How do I start?

Send a short note through the contact form. Keep it general, a sentence or two is plenty. I reply, usually within two business days, and if it looks like a fit we set up a short conversation at no cost.
