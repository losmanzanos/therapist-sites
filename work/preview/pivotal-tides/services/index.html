A Place to Begin

Individual Therapy

A space created for you

Individual therapy is a space created just for you, where your story is heard, your experiences are honored, and your growth is supported with intention and care. Life transitions, a lost sense of identity, relationship trouble, or the plain feeling of being stuck: therapy offers a chance to slow down, reflect, and reconnect with yourself in a meaningful way.

We begin with your story: how you got here, what matters most, and what tends to stand in your way. Tools, frameworks, and practices come after, shaped around the specifics of your life rather than handed down from a manual.

Most people arrive with one thing pressing on them. Here it is often grief, infertility, or the particular weight women carry, the areas Angelique focuses on most. She also sees people for anxiety and depression, burnout, anger, recovery from narcissistic abuse, and life during and after cancer.

Sessions are 50 minutes and $175, at the Temecula office or by secure video anywhere in California. Rates and insurance are listed in full.

Couples Therapy

Rebuilding connection

Through couples sessions, we'll explore the patterns, emotions, and experiences shaping your relationship, while building practical tools to help you move forward with clarity and confidence as a connected unit. The goal is understanding each other, strengthening your connection, and creating lasting change at a pace that feels right to both of you.

Both partners' experiences and perspectives are heard with equal weight, whether the problem is a specific conflict, a slow drift apart, infidelity, or a wish to deepen what is already good.

Couples sessions are 50 minutes and $200, in person or virtual. More on couples therapy and marriage counseling in Temecula, including premarital work.

Group Therapy

Connection through community

Group therapy can be a deeply healing experience, a reminder that you don't have to face life's challenges alone. In a supportive and thoughtfully guided space, you'll connect with others who share similar experiences, fostering a sense of understanding, validation, and community that can be difficult to find elsewhere.

Each group has its own focus and pace, with strict confidentiality as the foundation. Groups stay small, 6 to 8 people, so everyone has room to talk. Offerings vary seasonally; the Support Groups page covers what’s currently running and how to inquire about a fit.

Reproductive Support

Intended Parent & Surrogate Assessments

Third-party reproduction carries real emotional complexity, for the Intended Parent (IP) pursuing surrogacy and for the Gestational Carrier offering this extraordinary gift. Angelique provides clinical psychological evaluations and assessments required by fertility clinics and reproductive agencies, as well as ongoing mental health support throughout every stage of the process.

For Intended Parents (IPs): Psychological evaluations assessing emotional preparedness and readiness for surrogacy, along with supportive counseling through fertility treatments, donor decisions, and the waiting that comes with it.

For Gestational Carriers & Surrogates: Clinical assessments that meet the screening requirements of agencies and fertility clinics, plus ongoing support to protect your well-being through every stage of the surrogacy process.

This is an actively growing area of Angelique’s practice. Reach out to discuss availability, what the assessment process involves, and how ongoing support can be structured around your specific situation. Assessment fees aren’t listed online, so call (951) 395-6089 or send a message to ask. The infertility and surrogacy page goes into more detail.

Infertility often brings a kind of grief other people don’t see. Angelique writes about losses like that in Grief Doesn’t Follow a Timeline.

Before You Start

Questions people ask first

Can I see you in person in Temecula?

+

Yes. The office is at 43460 Ridge Park Dr, Suite 200A, in Temecula. Virtual sessions over secure, HIPAA-compliant video are available too, which helps if you’re coming from Murrieta or farther out in the Inland Empire. There’s a page with more for Murrieta clients.

How do I know which kind of therapy fits?

+

That’s what the free 15-minute consultation is for. Call (951) 395-6089 and talk it through. You can also start with individual therapy and add a group later, since groups are meant to sit alongside individual work.

What does a session cost?

+

Individual sessions are $175 and couples sessions are $200, each 50 minutes. Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross, Carelon and Cigna/Evernorth are accepted, along with out-of-network benefits. The rates page has the details, including the cancellation policy.

Can you do the psychological evaluation my surrogacy agency or clinic requires?

+

Angelique provides the psychological evaluations that fertility clinics and reproductive agencies ask for, for both Intended Parents and Gestational Carriers. Requirements differ from one agency to the next, so get in touch to check availability and go over what yours needs.
