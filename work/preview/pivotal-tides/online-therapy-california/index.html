Therapy by Video, Anywhere in California

Angelique Danza, LMFT, is licensed in California, so whether you’re in San Diego, Sacramento or a town with no therapist at all, sessions can happen from home.

How It Works

One license, the whole state

A California license covers anyone who is in California during the session. So the practice that sees people in person in Temecula also sees people by video in Los Angeles, Orange County, the Bay Area, the Central Valley and everywhere in between.

It’s the same work either way: depth-oriented, insight-focused, drawing on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Client-Centered Therapy. More on the approach

Sessions run over secure, HIPAA-compliant video. You need a private room and a steady connection. That’s it.

Common Questions

Before your first video session

Do I have to live near Temecula?

+

No. My license covers anyone in California, so we can meet by video wherever you are in the state. The Temecula office is there for people who are close and want to come in.

Can we meet while I’m traveling out of state?

+

No. Because I’m licensed in California, you need to be in California during the session. If you’ll be away, we can plan around it.

What do I need for a video session?

+

A private space where you won’t be overheard, a phone or computer with a camera, and a steady connection. Some people use a parked car. It works.

Do you take insurance?

+

Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross, Carelon and Cigna/Evernorth are accepted, along with out-of-network benefits. Paying directly, individual sessions are $175 and couples sessions $200, each 50 minutes.

How soon will I hear back?

+

Messages get a reply within 24 business hours. If you don’t hear back in that time, call (951) 395-6089.
