Getting Here

One office, on Ridge Park Drive

Pivotal Tides doesn’t have a Murrieta office. It has one office in Temecula, and from there the practice serves Temecula, Murrieta and the rest of the surrounding Inland Empire.

For most of Murrieta, that’s a short drive.

When the drive doesn’t fit the week, sessions happen over secure, HIPAA-compliant video instead. That goes for individual sessions and for couples, which matters when two work schedules leave no room for a commute.

Whichever you choose, it’s the same work with the same therapist: depth-oriented, insight-focused, drawing on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Client-Centered Therapy. More on the approach

What I Offer

Where to start

Individual therapy, $175 a session. My top focus areas are women’s issues, infertility, and grief. I also see people for anxiety and depression, burnout, anger, self-esteem, life transitions, recovery from narcissistic abuse, and life during and after cancer.

Couples therapy and marriage counseling, $200 a session. Conflict that keeps repeating, distance, infidelity, and premarital counseling for couples who want to talk the hard parts through before the wedding.

Grief counseling, for a death and for losses that never get a funeral.

Infertility counseling and surrogacy evaluations. I’m a Certified Women’s Mental Health & Reproductive Mental Health Specialist and provide the psychological evaluations fertility clinics and agencies require of Intended Parents and Gestational Carriers.

Common Questions

If you’re coming from Murrieta

Is there an office in Murrieta?

+

No. The one office is at 43460 Ridge Park Dr, Suite 200A, in Temecula, a short drive from Murrieta. Every kind of session is also available over secure, HIPAA-compliant video.

Can couples from Murrieta do sessions by video?

+

Yes. Couples sessions work in person or by video. Video helps when two work schedules don’t leave room for a drive.

Do you take insurance?

+

Aetna, Anthem Blue Cross, Carelon and Cigna/Evernorth are accepted, along with out-of-network benefits. Paying directly, individual sessions are $175 and couples sessions $200, each 50 minutes.

What happens on the free consultation?

+

It’s a 15-minute phone call. You say a little about what’s going on, ask whatever you want to ask, and we figure out together whether working with me is a good fit, and in which format.

How soon will I hear back?

+

Messages get a reply within 24 business hours. If you don’t hear back in that time, call (951) 395-6089.
