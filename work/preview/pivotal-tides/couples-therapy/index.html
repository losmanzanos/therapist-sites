Who Comes In

When the same argument keeps coming back

Couples usually call after the same fight has played out for the fortieth time, or after months of living politely side by side.

I work with couples stuck in a specific conflict, couples who feel disconnected, couples dealing with infidelity, and couples who are doing fine and want more. Premarital counseling is part of this work too, for partners who would rather talk the hard things through before the wedding than after it.

Family conflict and big life transitions tend to land on a relationship hard. They belong in the room as well.

In the Room

What a session looks like

Both of you are heard with equal weight. That sounds obvious. In a lot of couples, though, one person’s version of events has quietly become the official one, and the other has stopped arguing for theirs.

We look at the patterns, emotions, and experiences shaping the relationship. I draw on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Client-Centered Therapy, so some of the work is structural: the thoughts and beliefs that turn a passing comment into an attack. Some of it is slower, and makes room for the hurt or anger underneath that doesn’t have a tidy fix.

You’ll also build practical tools you can use at home right away, at a pace that works for both of you, including the partner who would rather go slow.

Nobody gets named the problem.

Common Questions

What couples ask before booking

How much does couples therapy cost?

+

$200 for a 50-minute session, paid at the time of service by card or check. Insurance and out-of-network details are on the rates page.

Can we do couples sessions online?

+

Yes. Couples sessions are available in person at the Temecula office or over secure, HIPAA-compliant video. Video helps when you’re coming from Murrieta, or when two work schedules don’t leave room for a drive.

Do you offer premarital counseling?

+

Yes. Marital and premarital counseling are both part of my practice. Nothing needs to be wrong for an engaged couple to come in.

Can couples therapy help after infidelity?

+

Infidelity is one of the areas I work with. I can’t promise an outcome, and no honest therapist can. What I can offer is a room where both of your experiences get equal weight while you work out what happened and what you want next.
