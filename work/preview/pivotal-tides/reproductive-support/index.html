Infertility

The part of treatment nobody puts on the calendar

Fertility treatment runs on appointments, lab results and waiting. What you feel about it keeps its own hours: hope at the start of a cycle, the drop when it ends, the strain it puts on a marriage, the pregnancy announcement you didn’t want to see.

Infertility is one of the three areas I focus on most. Counseling here covers fertility treatment, donor egg and donor sperm decisions, and the long stretches in between when nothing seems to be happening.

It is also a kind of grief other people often can’t see, and we can treat it as grief. More on grief counseling

If the strain is showing up between the two of you, couples therapy is part of the practice as well.

Third-Party Reproduction

Psychological evaluations for surrogacy

Fertility clinics and reproductive agencies ask both sides of a surrogacy arrangement for a psychological evaluation before things move forward. I provide those clinical evaluations and assessments, for Intended Parents and for Gestational Carriers, and ongoing mental health support afterward if you want it.

For Intended Parents (IPs)

An evaluation of emotional preparedness and readiness for surrogacy, done to your clinic’s or agency’s requirements.

Alongside it, or after, supportive counseling through fertility treatments, donor decisions, and the waiting that comes with all of it.

For Gestational Carriers & Surrogates

A clinical assessment that meets the screening requirements of your agency or fertility clinic.

Then, if you want it, ongoing support for your own well-being through every stage of the surrogacy process. Carrying a pregnancy for another family is generous and real work, and you deserve care of your own while you do it.

Requirements differ from one agency to the next. When you call, have your agency’s or clinic’s paperwork nearby so we can go over exactly what they need from you. This is an actively growing part of my practice.
