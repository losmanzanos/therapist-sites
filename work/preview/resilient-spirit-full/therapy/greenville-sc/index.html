Greenville

Specialty care without the waitlist or the drive.

If you have spent weeks calling therapists only to hear "we don't really do ERP" or "we're not taking new clients," you're not alone.

Clients join from Greenville, Greer, Simpsonville, Mauldin, Taylors, Travelers Rest, Easley and Spartanburg. The Upstate has a lot of college and high school athletes, and we hear from Clemson, Furman and Bob Jones students and their families about the same pressure: the standards, the scholarships, the feeling that one bad season changes everything.

Video sessions also mean no driving downtown on a weeknight. You can meet from your car in a parking lot if that's the only private place you have. Plenty of people do.
