Columbia

Familiar ground.

College towns run on pressure. Grades, playing time, Greek life, internships, and the feeling that everyone else has it figured out.

That is a big part of what we see from Columbia: students at USC, Columbia College and Benedict who look fine on paper and are falling apart in private, and the athletes who are carrying a scholarship and a team on top of it all. We also work with families across Lexington, Irmo, Chapin, Cayce, West Columbia, Blythewood and Forest Acres, from anxious grade-schoolers to parents trying to figure out how to help them.

Video sessions work around class schedules, practice and long commutes on I-20 and I-26.

FormatSecure video. You need to be in South Carolina or North Carolina during sessions.

Questions

Columbia specifics.

I am a student at USC. Can I see you instead of the counseling center?

Yes. Some students prefer a private therapist with specialty training in OCD, eating disorders or athlete concerns. Many student health plans are through the carriers we accept, and we will check your benefits first.

Which insurance do you take in Columbia?

In South Carolina: Aetna, Cigna and UnitedHealthcare. Self-pay is $150 per session. See fees and insurance.

Do you see children in Columbia?

Yes, by video, along with SPACE parent training for families of anxious kids. See teens, children and parents.
