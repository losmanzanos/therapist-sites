Mountain roads are optional.

A forty-five minute drive each way turns a fifty minute session into half a day. That is why a lot of people in the mountains stop going.

Clients join from Asheville, Black Mountain, Weaverville, Hendersonville, Arden, Candler and Waynesville, and from towns where finding a therapist trained in ERP or eating disorders has meant a long search.

Samantha is licensed in North Carolina as an LCSW, so you get the same care clients in Charleston get, with no road time.

Questions

Asheville specifics.

Are you licensed in North Carolina?

Yes. Samantha holds an LCSW in North Carolina (C018085) as well as her South Carolina license. You need to be physically in NC or SC during each session.

Which insurance do you take in Asheville?

In North Carolina: Aetna, Blue Cross NC, Cigna, Quest Behavioral Health and UnitedHealthcare. See fees and insurance.

Do I need anything special for video sessions?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera, a reasonably steady connection, and a private spot. We send the secure link before each session.
