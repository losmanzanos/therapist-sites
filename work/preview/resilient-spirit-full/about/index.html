About Samantha

Samantha McKenzie, therapist for OCD, eating disorders, anxiety and athletes

"Living with anxiety, OCD, or eating struggles is real, and it's not something you chose. The work can be challenging at times, but there is hope, and you don't have to do it alone."

In her words

Curiosity, not judgment.

Samantha wrote this for people who are on the fence about starting.

Did you know the therapeutic relationship can be one of the best predictors of positive treatment outcomes?

My role as your therapist is to help you find deeper meaning and understanding. Together, we look at the how and why behind your patterns, not with judgment, but with curiosity and compassion. Many of the behaviors you're struggling with once helped you get through difficult or overwhelming moments. If they were easy to stop, you would have done that already.

I help clients make sense of their patterns and understand why certain behaviors show up in their lives. We move at a pace that feels manageable, working toward change in ways that feel supportive rather than overwhelming. Your story is your own, and therapy isn't one-size-fits-all. We'll work together to help you move toward your goals and create a life that feels more fulfilling to you.

Along the way, we'll also work on softening perfectionism and quieting that inner critic, building more self-compassion as we go. Therapy can feel messy and uncomfortable, but often that's when healing and growth happen.

If you're feeling unsure, scared, or vulnerable about making changes, that makes sense. It's hard to let go of things that once helped you feel safe, even when they're no longer serving you.

Recovery is possible, and it's possible to build a life that aligns with your values.

The practice

Why "Resilient Spirit."

Most clients here look successful from the outside while struggling with self-doubt, performance pressure, burnout, body image or anxiety on the inside.

Resilient Spirit Counseling exists to help people find real freedom from anxiety, eating disorders, OCD, perfectionism and the pressure of high achievement.

The focus is on athletes, teens and adults who are holding a lot together. Through evidence-based care, clients build confidence, flexibility and resilience so they can do well in sport, work, relationships and everyday life.

The goal is practical: tools and skills that keep working long after therapy ends.
