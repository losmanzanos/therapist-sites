Who's reading this?

If you found this page on your own, that took something.

You don't have to have a name for what's going on. Maybe you can't stop thinking about food, or a mistake from last week's game, or a thought you'd never say out loud. Talking to someone who has heard it before helps more than people expect.

Your sessions are private. We'll explain exactly what gets shared with your parents before we start, and it's less than you think.

You know something is off, and you're tired of guessing what to do about it.

Maybe it's the stomachaches before school, the questions asked forty times, the meals that get smaller, or a kid who used to love their sport and now dreads it. You don't need to be sure it's "bad enough." Call, and we'll talk it through.

If your child won't come to therapy, you can still start. That's what SPACE is for.

Teen counseling

Teens who are overwhelmed and won't always say so.

Today's teens compare themselves to everyone, all the time, and many of them have decided that anything short of perfect is failure.

We help teens challenge the negative thoughts that keep them stuck and build confidence that doesn't depend on grades, likes or playing time. Sessions are by video, which most teens find easier than sitting across from an adult in an office.

Teens talk to their therapist privately. Parents are part of the intake and stay informed about the big picture and anything that affects safety, and we talk through what that looks like at the start so nobody is surprised.

Child counseling

Confidence and coping skills for younger kids.

Young children usually can't tell you they're anxious. You see it in the stomachaches before school, the meltdowns at bedtime, and the questions they ask again and again.

Our goal is to help children feel more confident and give them skills they can actually use when life gets hard. With kids, parents are closely involved. You are with your child far more than we are, so a lot of the work happens through you.

SPACE for parents

When the anxious one won't come to therapy.

SPACE (Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions) was developed by Dr. Eli Lebowitz at the Yale Child Study Center. It works through parents, so your child doesn't have to participate at all.

  1. Spot the accommodations

    When a child is anxious, families start working around it. Answering the same question again, sleeping in their room, ordering for them at restaurants. It comes from love, and it keeps the anxiety going.

  2. Say it supportively

    You learn to show your child that you understand how hard this is and that you are confident they can handle it. Both at once.

  3. Step back, one at a time

    Together we pick one accommodation to reduce, plan exactly how, and prepare for the pushback. Then the next one.

  4. Watch them surprise you

    Kids build resilience by finding out they can cope. SPACE has strong research support for childhood anxiety and OCD.

Questions

What parents ask first.

Something else on your mind? Ask it on the free call, or text (843) 212-6965.

How young are the children you see?

Ask us on the consult call. The right fit depends on your child's age and what is going on, and for younger kids, parent-focused work like SPACE is often where we start.

Do I need to be in the session?

For younger children, often yes, at least part of the time. For teens, sessions are usually one-on-one with parent check-ins. With SPACE, it is only you.

Will you tell me what my teen says?

Not word for word. Teens open up when they trust their sessions are private. You will hear about overall progress and anything involving their safety, and we agree on the details together at intake.

Do you take insurance for children?

Yes, the same plans we accept for adults. See fees and insurance.
