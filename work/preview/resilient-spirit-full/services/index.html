By age

What we see at each stage.

Initial intake evaluation $160, individual sessions $150, at every age. Most plans are accepted; check yours.

Children

Anxiety disorders, OCD, ADHD, depression, perfectionism and low self-esteem. Parents are closely involved, and SPACE is available when a child won't come in.

Teens

Anxiety, OCD, eating disorders, body image, athletes and performance anxiety, perfectionism and burnout, trauma, depression, ADHD and low self-esteem.

Adults

Anxiety, OCD, eating disorders, body image, athletes and performance anxiety, perfectionism and burnout, trauma, depression, ADHD and relationships.

Parents

SPACE (Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions) teaches you what to change at home so your child's anxiety has less room to grow.
