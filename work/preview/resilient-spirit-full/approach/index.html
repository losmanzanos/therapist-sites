Our approach: evidence-based therapy with ERP, CBT, ACT and SPACE

The relationship comes first. The evidence decides the rest.

Research consistently finds that the relationship between client and therapist is one of the strongest predictors of whether therapy works. So we put real effort there. Then we use the treatments with the best evidence for your specific problem.

The treatments

Four methods, each matched to a specific problem.

ERPOCD

Exposure and Response Prevention

The gold-standard treatment for OCD. You confront the thoughts, images, objects and situations you usually avoid, gradually and in a planned order, and practice not doing the compulsion that follows. Over time the anxiety drops on its own, and the thoughts lose their power.

Good fit forOCD in all its forms, health anxiety, phobias, and the fear foods and rituals that come with eating disorders. Read more
CBTAnxiety

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Focuses on how your thoughts, feelings and actions relate to each other. You learn to recognize unhelpful thinking patterns, like catastrophizing, mind-reading and all-or-nothing standards, and practice new ones that are more accurate and more useful.

Good fit forAnxiety, depression, perfectionism, low self-esteem, eating disorders and performance anxiety. Read more
ACTValues

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy

Uses acceptance and mindfulness to build psychological flexibility. Instead of trying to win every argument with your own head, you learn to let difficult thoughts and feelings be there while you act on what matters to you.

Good fit forBurnout, body image, identity outside of sport, chronic worry, and anyone tired of fighting their thoughts. Read more
SPACEParents

Supportive Parenting for Anxious Childhood Emotions

Developed by Dr. Eli Lebowitz. Parents learn to spot the ways the family has accommodated a teen's anxiety or OCD, and to reduce those accommodations slowly and supportively so the teen builds their own resilience and confidence.

Good fit forParents of anxious or OCD-affected teens, including teens who refuse to come to therapy. Read more

What to expect

From first call to last session.

Therapy here has a direction. You will know what we are working on and how we will tell if it's working.

  1. Free 15 minute call

    What's going on, what you have tried, and whether we're the right fit. If we're not, we'll help you find who is.

  2. Intake evaluation

    A longer first session on secure video. We get the full picture and agree on goals in plain language.

  3. Weekly sessions

    Most clients meet weekly at first. Sessions are active: you leave with something to practice before next time.

  4. Practice between sessions

    Change happens in your actual life, not only on the call. Exposures, thought records and values work happen in between.

  5. Wrapping up

    As things improve we space sessions out and build a plan for setbacks, so you leave with skills that keep working.

Questions about any of this?

Ask on the free call. We are happy to explain how a treatment would work for your situation before you commit to anything.
