Before you reach out

A few practical things.

Current clients can message, schedule and pay through the client portal.

Do I have to live in South Carolina or North Carolina?

You need to be physically in SC or NC at the time of each session, because that is where Samantha is licensed. Visiting from another state for the session does not count, but living elsewhere and being here for school or work does.

What should I put in the form?

Just enough to start: your name, how to reach you, and a sentence about what is going on. Please keep detailed health information out of the form and save it for the call.

How fast will I hear back?

Within one business day. Texting (843) 212-6965 is usually the fastest way to reach us.

What if I am in crisis?

This form is not monitored around the clock. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), call 911, or go to your nearest emergency room.
