Brooke Bloker, LPC, LMHC

I’m a licensed therapist in Fort Collins, working with adults who live with complex trauma and dissociation. I see people in person on South Timberline Road and by telehealth in Colorado, Iowa, and Arizona.

Where I come from

Before private practice, I spent years in places where things were rarely tidy.

I worked in outpatient care and in a women’s inpatient program, supporting mothers through substance use and trauma, often while they were working to get their kids back. You learn a lot about survival in a place like that. Mostly, you learn that the things people are most ashamed of usually started out as solutions.

I’ve been in this field for more than seven years. My practice now centers on trauma and dissociation, especially for people who feel stuck in patterns that don’t fully make sense to them or feel out of their control. I also work with mood and anxiety concerns, codependency, and personality-related challenges, including BPD, always through a trauma-informed lens.

What it’s like to work with me

I’m practical and fairly direct. I’ll tell you what I’m noticing and why I’m suggesting something, and you can always say no.

A first session with me is mostly me listening. I want to know what’s happening now, what you’ve already tried, and what you’d want to be different in six months. I’ll ask about the hard stuff only as far as you want to go. Nobody has to prove anything to be here.

After that, I’m not a blank wall. If I think we’re avoiding something, I’ll say so. If I think we’re moving too fast, I’ll say that too, and I say it more often. The slow part is where most of the change happens.

A few things I believe

  • The patterns you’re most ashamed of usually started as solutions.
  • The parts of you that react “too much” are responding to real information. It’s just old.
  • You don’t need a diagnosis, a clear memory, or a good explanation to start.
  • Stability isn’t the waiting room before the real work. It is the real work, for a long while.
  • You set the pace. I’ll tell you what I think, and then it’s your call.

I see adults, and my practice is LGBTQIA+, trans and nonbinary, polyamory and CNM, and BIPOC affirming. The specifics of what I offer are on the services page.
