How sessions work

Sessions run 53 minutes or more. Most people come weekly to start.

Your first appointment is a 60-minute intake. We go over what’s bringing you in, what you’ve tried, and what you want to be different. After that, we settle into a rhythm. Some people move to every other week once things are steadier.

In-person sessions are at my office at 3726 South Timberline Road in Fort Collins. Telehealth sessions happen over secure video, anywhere in Colorado, Iowa, or Arizona. EMDR works by telehealth too.

Mac, my golden retriever, is in the office most days. If you’d rather he wait next door, just say so.

Where to start

You don’t have to pick. This is how it usually sorts itself out.

“Something keeps happening and I can’t explain it.”

Start with individual therapy. We figure out the pattern first. If trauma is underneath it, EMDR or parts work comes in once you have solid ground to stand on.

“I know exactly what happened. I just can’t get past it.”

That’s often an EMDR case, though we still spend the first stretch on preparation. One clear memory tends to go faster than years of them, and both are workable.

“I lose time, or I feel like different people on different days.”

Dissociation and parts work. We go slower on purpose, and nothing gets processed until the parts of you that carry it agree.

“I got out. Why do I still miss them?”

Relational trauma recovery. Trauma bonds are strong for reasons that make sense, and understanding them is the first step out.
