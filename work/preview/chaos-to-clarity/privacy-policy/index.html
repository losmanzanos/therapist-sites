Privacy policy

What this website collects, what it doesn’t, and where your information goes.

Last updated October 2026

What this covers

This policy covers chaostoclarityllc.com only. Your therapy records are protected by HIPAA and covered by a separate Notice of Privacy Practices, which you’ll receive at your first session.

What the site collects

If you use the contact form, the site collects what you type into it: your name, email address, phone number, state, and message. That information is sent to Brooke’s inbox through EmailJS, a form delivery service, and is used only to respond to you.

Please keep health details out of the form. Email and web forms aren’t secure channels for sensitive information.

What the site doesn’t do

This site doesn’t use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, and it doesn’t sell or share your information with anyone. Fonts and images are served from this site, so simply visiting doesn’t send your information to third parties.

Like most websites, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, for security and reliability.

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites, like crisis lines or professional organizations. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

This site is intended for adults and isn’t directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change too.
