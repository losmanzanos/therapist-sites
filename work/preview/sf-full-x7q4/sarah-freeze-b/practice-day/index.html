What we do

We start with a short review of the eight phases, using the EMDR Fidelity Checklist. Then it’s role-play, and you sit in both chairs: clinician and client.

  • Full protocol setup
  • Phases 4 through 7
  • Restricted processing
  • Parts work when processing is blocked

Why come

Most people finish training knowing the protocol on paper and feeling shaky in the room. A day of repetitions, with someone watching and correcting, closes that gap faster than reading does.

You run the protocol with a colleague as your client, and I correct as you go.
