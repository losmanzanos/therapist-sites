I train agencies and group practices on EMDR, attachment trauma, and working with adults who were abused or neglected as children.

Ask about a trainingonline, or i come to you
Agencies + groups

What I can teach

  • EMDR for complex trauma and insecure attachment
  • Treating adult survivors of childhood abuse and neglect
  • Bringing ego state, somatic, and CBT tools into trauma work
  • Rejection trauma and relational wounds
  • Adapting EMDR to telehealth

A full day works well for a team this size.

Denver Art Museum, Hamilton Building. Training happens online, or in person by arrangement.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your clinicians and what they’re running into.
  2. I write back with what a day or a series could look like.
  3. We set dates. I teach online, and in person by arrangement.

Why me

I’ve worked in community agencies with children and adults affected by sexual abuse, sexual assault, physical abuse, and domestic violence. Before private practice I was a clinical director. I know what an agency caseload looks like.

I’m also a PESI presenter, so the material has been taught to large rooms and revised more than once.

After the training

Some teams want a standing consultation hour once the training is over, so the new skills survive contact with real cases. I offer that too.
