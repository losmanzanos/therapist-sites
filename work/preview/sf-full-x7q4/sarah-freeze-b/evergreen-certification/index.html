What we cover

We review the basics of EMDR, work with a case formulation tool, and spend most of our time on your cases.

At least once, you’ll bring a verbatim transcript or a recording of a session. That’s where the learning is.

Who it’s for

Clinicians who have enrolled in or finished an EMDR training, most often through PESI or Psychotherapy Networker, and want to become an Evergreen Certified EMDR Clinician.

How certification works

  1. Complete your 10 hours of group consultation here.
  2. Complete 6 clinical practice hours. A live practice day covers them.
  3. Email me. I sign your paperwork and send it back.

Evergreen lists the full requirements, and I recorded a short video that walks through the process.

Why Evergreen

I partnered with PESI to bring their EMDR training up to a standard I’d put beside any other. As part of that, I was asked to give up my EMDRIA consultant status.

Certification and consultant status through me now run through PESI’s partner, Evergreen Certifications.
