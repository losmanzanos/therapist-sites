EMDR consultation and training for therapists who treat complex trauma.

I’m an Evergreen-approved EMDR consultant and a PESI presenter. Most of my own clients are adults who were abused or neglected as children.

I can help you run the standard protocol well. I can also help when a case needs more than the standard protocol. Everything meets on Zoom, so you can join from any state.

How I work

I trained in EMDR in 2018 and was mentored by Arielle Schwartz. Since then I’ve brought parts work, the body, and psychodynamic thinking into it.

In consultation I pay attention to the parts of therapy that are harder to name, like what you feel toward a client and what that might be telling you.
