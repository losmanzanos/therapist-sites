About

How I work

Trained in New York. Practicing in New York. Denver.

Sarah Freeze, LCSW

Harlem, New York. Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress.

I’m a psychotherapist in private practice in Denver. I specialize in trauma, and most of my clients are adults who lived through childhood abuse, neglect, or not being seen.

In the therapy room

I use EMDR, psychodynamic psychotherapy, and sand tray. My framework is person-centered and culturally informed, which includes naming racism, sexism, and heteronormativity when they’re part of what hurt someone.

In consultation

I can help you carry out the standard EMDR protocol well. I can also help you think through a complex case that needs a more progressive approach. That work is relational, and it draws on ego state theory, somatic psychology, and structural dissociation.

Transference and countertransference get a lot of my attention, including the intense feelings a clinician can have toward a client. Those are easy to leave out of a case presentation, and they tend to matter.

People I consult with have described me as knowledgeable, warm, and responsive. I’ll take it.

Who shaped my thinking

Arielle Schwartz, PhD, was my mentor. I’ve also learned a great deal from Jim Knipe, Robin Shapiro, Dolores Mosquera, and Janina Fisher.

PESI, Evergreen, and EMDRIA

I partnered with PESI to bring their EMDR training up to a standard I’d put beside any other. As part of that, I was asked to give up my EMDRIA consultant status and certification.

If you pursue EMDR certification or consultant status with me, it runs through PESI’s partner, Evergreen Certifications.
