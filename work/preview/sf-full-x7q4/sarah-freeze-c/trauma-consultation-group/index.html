What it’s like

The group is collaborative, and every approach is welcome. My own lenses are EMDR, psychodynamic therapy, and sand tray, so those shape how I listen.

You bring cases. So does everyone else. Over a few months you start to know each other’s clients almost as well as your own.

Why it isn’t drop-in

A committed group can pick up where it left off. People remember what you tried last month and ask how it went.
