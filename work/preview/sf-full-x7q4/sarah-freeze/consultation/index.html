How I consult

I can help you run the standard EMDR protocol well. I can also help when a case needs more than the standard protocol, which with complex trauma is most of them.

My approach is relational. It draws on ego state theory, somatic psychology, and structural dissociation, and I pay close attention to transference and countertransference.
