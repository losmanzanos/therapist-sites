What we can work on

  • A complex case where the standard protocol keeps stalling
  • Blocked processing, and what to try next
  • Bringing parts work or somatic work into EMDR
  • Dissociation, and how to pace around it
  • What you feel toward a client, and what to do with it

How I think

My approach is integrative. I draw on parts work, somatic psychology, psychodynamic theory, and relational psychotherapy.

I also listen for transference and countertransference. Strong feelings toward a client are information, and consultation is a good place to say them out loud.
