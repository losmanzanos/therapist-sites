Curiosity no. 01 · Anxiety and stress

Why can’t I relax when everything is fine?

Therapy for anxiety and stress in Denver

Nothing is wrong, exactly. The job is fine, the kids are fine, and you still wake at 3 a.m. running through a list.

Book a 15-minute call with Lora

It’s 3 a.m. Move the light around the room.

What it tends to look like

  • You finish one thing and your mind is already on the next three.
  • Rest feels like something you have to earn first.
  • Your shoulders are up by your ears, and you only notice when someone points it out.
  • Small decisions take an hour because every option could be the wrong one.

What we do about it

Lora starts with what the worry is doing for you. Most anxiety began as a good idea: stay alert, stay ahead, don’t get caught off guard. It worked, and then it never switched off.

Sessions move between two things. One is practical: sleep, the 3 a.m. loop, what your body does before your mind catches up. The other is slower. It’s about where you learned that being at ease wasn’t allowed.

She draws on psychodynamic and attachment-based therapy, along with mindfulness skills you can use the same day.

How long it takes

Some people come for a few months with one thing to sort out. Others stay longer because the anxiety turned out to be attached to something older.

You and Lora decide that together. You can ask her at any point how she thinks it’s going.

Questions people ask

Is this anxiety or just stress?

It may not matter much. If it’s getting in the way of sleep, work, or the people you love, it’s worth an hour of talking it through.

Do you prescribe medication?

No. Lora is a therapist and doesn’t prescribe. If medication seems worth a conversation, she can help you think it through and coordinate with your doctor.

Can we meet online?

Yes. Lora sees clients at the Harlan Street office and by video anywhere in Colorado.
