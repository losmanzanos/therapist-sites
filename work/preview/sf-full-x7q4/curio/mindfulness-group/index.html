Curiosity no. 10 · Mindfulness skills group

Could eight weeks make my brain quieter?

Mindfulness skills group, online in Colorado

Stress Relief: Mindfulness Skills for Everyday Life is an eight-week online group led by Lora Hope. It’s closer to a class than a therapy group. You learn skills, practice them, and take them home.

What happens in a session

Each week has a short teaching piece, a guided practice, and time to try the skill and ask questions. You’ll learn how your own stress response works and build a small set of tools you can use in the middle of a regular day.

Nobody is asked to share more than they want to. Plenty of people mostly listen.

Eight weeks of noticing what is already on the table.

Hover the picture to light it

This might be for you if

  • You feel stressed, overwhelmed, or worn out most days.
  • You get stuck in worry, overthinking, or self-criticism.
  • You react first and wish you’d paused.
  • You want something practical.

How to join

Fill out the short interest form. It asks which start date and time of day suit you, and it doesn’t commit you to anything. Lora will follow up about scheduling and registration.

Questions? Write to lora@sarahfreezelcsw.com or call (720) 465-1253.

Questions people ask

Is this group therapy?

No. It’s skills-based and closer to a class.

Do I need to have meditated before?

No.
