If your plan isn’t listed

We can give you a superbill to send to your insurer for out-of-network reimbursement. Before you start, it helps to call the number on your card and ask:

  • Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits?
  • Is there a deductible, and how much of it have I met?
  • What do you reimburse per session once it’s met?
  • Is there a limit on the number of sessions?

If they ask for a procedure code, it’s 90834.

Good Faith Estimate

If you aren’t using insurance, you have the right to a Good Faith Estimate of what your care will cost. We provide it in writing at least one business day before your first session, and you can ask for one before you schedule.

If a bill comes in $400 or more above your estimate, you can dispute it. Keep a copy of the estimate. More at cms.gov/nosurprises, or from the Colorado Division of Insurance at 303-894-7490.

Practice forms

The Notice of Privacy Practices, Mandatory Disclosure, Practice Policies, and Release of Information are sent before your first session. Ask if you’d like to read them sooner.
