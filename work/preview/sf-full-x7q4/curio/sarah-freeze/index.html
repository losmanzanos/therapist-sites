What I do

I’m a psychotherapist, and I’ve spent most of my career with adults who were abused or neglected as children. My main tools are EMDR, psychodynamic psychotherapy, and sand tray.

I also work with anxiety and phobias, depression, chronic illness and pain, major life transitions, and people who grew up in alcoholic or chaotic families.

Where things stand

My weekly practice is full. I still see people for EMDR intensives, I supervise the clinical work at Curio, and I teach and consult with other therapists.

If you’re looking for weekly therapy, Lora Hope is taking new clients. I brought her on so the door could stay open.

Consultation and training

Training

I finished the two-year Adult Psychodynamic Training Program at the Denver Institute for Psychoanalysis in 2024. I began EMDR training in 2018, and I trained in trauma-focused CBT through the National Child Traumatic Stress Network’s Child and Adolescent Trauma Program.

Before private practice I was clinical director at Create Outcomes Psychological Services. I have a BA in psychology from SUNY Geneseo and an MSW from the Silberman School of Social Work at Hunter College. I also worked on federally funded research at Stony Brook University and the University of Rochester.
