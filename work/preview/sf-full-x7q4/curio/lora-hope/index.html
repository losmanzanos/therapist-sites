Lora Hope, LSW

Lora works with women whose lives look put-together from the outside and feel like too much from the inside.

Who she works with

Lora sees adults, and most of her clients are women who are good at holding things together. They run teams, households, or both. They come in during a divorce, a career change, menopause, or the months after the last kid leaves home, and often they say some version of: I should be able to handle this.

She also works with stress and trauma, depression, relationships, and the perfectionism that tends to travel with high achievement.

How she works

Her training is humanistic, psychodynamic, and attachment-based, with mindfulness skills alongside. In plain terms, she pays attention to patterns and she’s curious about where they started.

You can ask her questions and tell her when something isn’t working. That’s how the practice runs.

Training

Lora holds a master’s in social work from Fordham University, on the clinical practice track, with more than 900 hours of supervised field work. Her bachelor’s degree is from Pace University.

She is a Licensed Social Worker in Colorado (LSW.0009927221) and practices under the clinical supervision of Sarah Freeze, LCSW. She is completing training in EMDR.
