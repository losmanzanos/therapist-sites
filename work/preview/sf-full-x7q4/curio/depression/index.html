Curiosity no. 06 · Depression

Why does everything feel so heavy?

Therapy for depression in Denver

You’re getting through the day. You’re also going through the motions, and things you used to love feel like items on a list.

From the inside

  • Sleep is too much or not nearly enough.
  • You cancel plans and feel both relieved and worse.
  • Your inner voice is harsher with you than you’d ever be with a friend.
  • People tell you to look on the bright side and you want to throw something.

How Lora works with it

Depression can be mild and grinding, or it can take the floor out from under you. Either way the first job is understanding yours: when it started, what it’s attached to, what makes a day slightly better or worse.

Lora works with mood and depression in adults using psychodynamic and humanistic therapy. In practice you get someone on your side in the present while you start to trace where the heaviness comes from.

If medication might help, she can coordinate with your prescriber or help you find one.

If it’s urgent

If you’re thinking about ending your life, call or text 988 at any hour, or go to the nearest emergency room. Therapy can help, and it shouldn’t be your only line in a crisis.

Questions people ask

How do I know if it’s depression or a rough patch?

Two weeks is the usual rule of thumb. If low mood or loss of interest has lasted longer than that and it’s affecting your life, come talk.

Do you take insurance for this?

Yes. Curio is in network with Kaiser, UnitedHealthcare, and several others. The fees page has the full list.
