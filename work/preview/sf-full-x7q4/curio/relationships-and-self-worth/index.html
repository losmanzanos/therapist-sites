Relationships and self-worth

Sound familiar?

  • You’re the one everybody leans on, and you’re not sure who you lean on.
  • You apologize first, even when you’re not the one who’s wrong.
  • Getting close feels good for a while and then it feels dangerous.
  • You can list your accomplishments and still feel like you’re not enough.

What therapy does with it

How you learned to attach to people as a child tends to show up in every relationship after. Once you can see the pattern, you have some say in it.

Lora works relationally, which means the relationship between the two of you is part of the work. If you tend to hide what you need, that will probably happen with her too. Then it becomes something you can look at together while it’s happening.

Self-worth usually improves as a side effect. It’s hard to keep believing you’re too much, or not enough, when someone has seen all of it and stayed.

Questions people ask

Is this individual or couples therapy?

Individual. You come on your own, and the focus is your side of the pattern, which is the side you can change.

What does attachment-based mean?

It means paying attention to how your earliest relationships taught you to expect closeness to go, and how that shows up now.
