Telehealth

Online therapy from anywhere in Colorado.

Every session happens over Zoom for Healthcare, so the commute from Durango is the same as the commute from Capitol Hill: none.

I’m a Licensed Professional Counselor and Licensed Addiction Counselor in Colorado, and the whole practice is virtual. If you’re physically in Colorado at the time of the session, I can see you.

That covers a lot of ground. The Front Range, from Fort Collins down to Pueblo. Mountain towns where the closest counselor might be over a pass. The Western Slope, from Grand Junction to Durango. The Eastern Plains, where an hour each way to an office isn’t unusual.

Same rates and the same counselor, wherever you log in from.

What you need on your end

  • A private spot where you can talk freely. A home office works. So does a bedroom with the door shut, or a parked car between obligations.
  • A working internet connection and a phone or computer with a camera
  • To be in Colorado for the session itself. If you’ll be traveling out of state on a session day, tell me ahead of time.

Common questions

About online sessions

Do you offer in-person sessions?

Not right now. Currently, all sessions are conducted virtually via Zoom for Healthcare.

Can an assessment be done online?

Yes. The substance use and mental health assessment is one 60–90 minute telehealth session, with written findings afterward. If a court or employer referred you, check whether they have any requirements about how the evaluation is done.

How private is a video session?

The platform is Zoom for Healthcare, and your side of the privacy is the room you pick. A closed door and headphones go a long way. So does a parked car.

How soon can I start?

Same-week openings are available. Send a note with a few windows that work for you and I’ll reply within one business day with times that match.
