The team

Four therapists. Real people.

All trained in trauma-informed care. All warm, grounded, and glad you reached out.

Practice owner · LPC, EMDR-trained

Katie Fortunato

Works with: complex trauma, EMDR, dissociation, parts work, perfectionism, identity, and the high-functioning version of "I'm fine."

Trauma therapist and EMDR enthusiast. Clients describe her as funny, grounding, and weirdly calming. She can sit with heavy things without making them heavier, and will use a bit of dark humor if it helps you feel less alone in the room.

  • EMDR
  • Complex trauma
  • Dissociation
  • Parts work
  • Perfectionism
  • Anxiety
  • Identity
Meet Katie
Pre-licensed therapist · LPCC

Alissa Brown

Works with: kids and teens, anxiety, military families, parenting, school stress, and the postpartum stretch nobody warned you about.

Clients describe her as warm, calm, and grounded. She's a listener first. She won't perform analysis at you. She'll just be in it with you while you sort through whatever's loud right now.

  • Children & teens
  • Military families
  • Anxiety
  • Play therapy
  • Parenting
  • Depression
Meet Alissa
Licensed therapist · LPC

Ebony Trotter

Works with: teens and adults working through anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, ADHD, self-esteem, relationships, and the life transitions that knock you sideways.

Clients describe her as warm, genuine, and easy to talk to. She blends empathy with gentle honesty, draws from an integrative, trauma-informed toolkit, and makes room for laughter, because therapy doesn't have to feel intimidating to change things.

  • Anxiety
  • Depression
  • Trauma & grief
  • ADHD
  • Self-esteem
  • Life transitions
Meet Ebony
Pre-licensed therapist · LPCC · Online only

Cameron Bice

Works with: teens 15 and up and adults dealing with complex trauma, intimate partner violence, substance use, neurodivergence, chronic illness, and big life changes. Military families too.

Friends call Cameron bold, driven, and outspoken, and honest to a fault. Sessions are client-paced and direct, with room for humor, a little quiet when things have been loud, and Brainspotting or EMDR when it fits.

  • Complex trauma
  • Intimate partner violence
  • Substance use
  • Neurodivergence
  • Chronic illness
  • Brainspotting
Meet Cameron
Practice coordinator

Savanna Schweizer

Savanna keeps things running behind the scenes at Crescita, so the therapists can stay focused on the people in front of them. She came here from admin work at a chiropractic clinic, a university graduate program, and an elementary school, and she's happiest in a role that keeps the details organized and the communication clear.

Outside of work, you'll find her with her husband and daughter (outside, ideally), out with friends, trying a new recipe or coffee shop, or thrifting.
