The approach

We don't do one-size-fits-all.

Whether you're carrying decades of old pain, stuck in a loop you can't think your way out of, or parenting a kid who's struggling to say what's wrong, there's a path here. We match the modality to what you actually need, not the other way around.

i.

Nervous system first

We don't push faster than your system can go. Real work happens when your body feels safe enough to let the mind follow.

ii.

No performance required

Swear, cry, go blank, contradict yourself. You don't have to arrive with your thoughts organized. That's what the room is for.

iii.

In-person or virtual

We see clients face-to-face in downtown Colorado Springs, and virtually anywhere in Colorado. Most people do a mix of both.
