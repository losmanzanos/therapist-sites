ADHD & neurodivergence · Colorado Springs + virtual

ADHD therapy that isn't about fixing your brain.

For teens and adults who are tired of being told to just try harder.

Book a free 20-min call

Licensed counselors · In person on N Weber St · Virtual across Colorado · Sliding scale

If you have ADHD, you've probably heard it all. Lazy. Too much. So smart if you'd only apply yourself. A lot of people arrive at therapy carrying years of that, and the shame is often heavier than the ADHD itself.

We don't treat neurodivergence as a defect. The goal is to understand how your brain actually works, build a life that fits it, and deal with the anxiety, burnout and relationship strain that pile up when you've spent years forcing yourself into systems built for someone else.

You don't need a formal diagnosis to start. This page is about therapy, not testing.

What we work with

The ADHD part, and everything around it.

  • Time blindness and overwhelm
  • Years of "lazy" and "too much"
  • Masking and burnout
  • Diagnosed later in life
  • Big emotions, fast
  • Rejection that hits hard
  • School and work
  • Partners and roommates
  • Anxiety and depression riding along

Autistic, ADHD, both, or still figuring it out. You're welcome either way.

Who you'd work with

Two therapists, two ways in.

Ebony Trotter, LPC works with ADHD in teens and adults, along with the anxiety, depression and self-esteem pieces that often come with it. Ebony sees clients in person at our office on N Weber St and online anywhere in Colorado.

Cameron Bice, LPCC works with neurodivergence in teens 15 and up and adults, online only, anywhere in Colorado. Cameron's style is direct and client-paced, with no expectation that you show up a certain way.

For kids under 15, start with child and teen therapy. Rates, insurance and the sliding scale are on the rates page.

Start with a call. Fidgeting allowed.

A free 20-minute call. Tell us what's been hard, and we'll help you pick between Ebony and Cameron.
