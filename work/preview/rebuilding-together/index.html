From Amy, who started this

"I believe you can be both falling apart and rising strong, and I'm here for all of it."

I went back to grad school at 43, as a single mom, because I needed a career that meant something and paid the bills. I launched this practice in the middle of real life: Covid, perimenopause, parenting pre-teens, a blended family of five neurodivergent humans and four needy rescue dogs. The practice is rooted in radical honesty, deep empathy, fierce vulnerability, and a whole lot of swearing.
