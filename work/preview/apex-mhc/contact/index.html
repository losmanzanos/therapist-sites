Start with fifteen minutes.

A short call, no paperwork and no obligation, to work out whether this is a fit. If it is not, I will say so and point you toward someone who is a better match.

Whatever you send stays confidential, and I usually reply within one business day. Please keep clinical detail brief here. Email is convenient, not secure, and the full picture is better handled on the call.
