Selected work

Eight practices, a property manager and a timber framer. No two sites alike.

Every project below was hand-coded from a blank file for one practice or business, starting from who its clients are. There is no shared template underneath any of them, and each client owns their domain, hosting and code outright.

fullbloomcounseling.com
Group practice

Full Bloom Counseling Group

Denver, Colorado

A growing Denver group practice of seven clinicians needed a website that could hold every therapist's story without collapsing into a faceless directory. The goal was a site that felt like the practice itself and got each client to the right therapist quickly.

What I built: individual clinician profiles, specialty service pages, JSON-LD structured data for local search, analytics, and a contact form with multiple delivery fallbacks so no inquiry ever gets lost. Hand-coded and deployed on edge hosting at no monthly cost.

apex-mhc.com
Brand identity & website

Apex Mental Health Counseling

Colorado, by telehealth

Dr. Jeremy Reichert, PhD, LPC, NCC, had fourteen years of clinical practice behind him and needed the whole identity, not just a website. He works with veterans, survivors of domestic violence, people in recovery, burned-out high achievers, and couples, all by telehealth across Colorado. The identity had to feel steady enough for that whole list without looking like a clinic.

What I built: the identity came first. The mark is two nested peaks, the name is set in Bodoni Moda, and the palette is a quiet ink, paper, and sage. From there, a logo kit in every shape and color he'd need, press-ready business cards, and a branded inquiry email. The site carries real Colorado contour lines through as its texture, gives every specialty its own page, and has a journal Jeremy writes himself from a simple editor, with a switch that keeps drafts invisible until he publishes.

Lovett Counseling & Consulting

Denver, Colorado

A trauma-informed counselor (LPC, CCTP) building a practice around depth and pacing needed a site that read the way her sessions feel: unhurried. The writing mattered as much as the design.

What I built: a full multi-page site, separate pages for individual therapy, couples therapy, and consulting & training, plus a fees page, an FAQ, and an about page, each with its own structured data and local SEO, wrapped in an editorial voice built to turn a quiet reader into a first phone call.

goldenhourwellnesscolorado.com
Group practice

Golden Hour Wellness Colorado

Colorado

Founder Shayla Martinez-O'Brien runs a group practice that, among other things, hosts an ongoing support group for new mothers, one whose meeting dates shift from month to month. A site that needed a developer on call for every date change wasn't going to work.

What I built: a full migration off Squarespace onto hand-coded, edge-hosted pages, blog/journal functionality, simple self-edit access so the practice can update group dates and page copy themselves, and a combined scheduling & insurance widget, Acuity and Thrizer wired together into one toggle instead of two separate embeds competing for the same space on the page.

Squarespace migration Blog / Journal Self-edit content access Acuity + Thrizer widget Structured data $0 hosting

belongconsultingco.com
Shopify redesign & product photography

Belong Consulting

Denver, Colorado

Becca Moravec, MA, LPC, LMFT runs Belong alongside Full Bloom: Enneagram coaching, workshops, consultation for therapists, and Sad Girls Club, a merch line “for anyone who feels things.” The store was a stock Shopify theme with Christmas flat-lays still up in October.

What I built: a custom Shopify theme with 18 sections and templates, a homepage moon that shows the real phase over Denver tonight, 47 product photos restyled into night scenes and to-scale size guides, and the SEO Shopify leaves out. Theme images dropped from 4.7 MB to 0.8 MB.

Rooted Resonance Therapy

Colorado

Annie Memmott had built real search equity under her original name, Rooted Resonance Coaching, then decided the practice needed a different name and license to match what it actually was: somatic therapy and sound healing, not coaching. A full rebrand, not a redesign.

What I built: a new hand-coded site under the new domain and the new name, plus 301 redirects from every URL on the old coaching site, so none of that earned SEO authority got orphaned the day the old domain went dark.

Also: timber framing

Log Dog Timberworks

Log Dog hand-cuts timber frame pergolas, pavilions, arbors and hewn beams for backyards across the Denver metro, in solid Douglas fir joined with mortise and tenon and oak pegs. The joinery is the whole pitch, and it doesn’t come through in a phone photo, so the site had to show the craft up close and still turn up when someone in Arvada or Castle Rock searches for a pergola builder.

What I built: a full site with a page for each kind of build, a service-area page covering sixteen Front Range towns, project write-ups in a journal, and structured data naming every town served, hand-coded and deployed with the same zero-platform-fee setup as everything else here.
