Hi! I'm Amy, the owner and founder. I'm so glad you're here.

Rebuilding Together Counseling isn't just a practice, it's a purpose. I went back to grad school at 43, as a single mom, because I had to find a career that was meaningful to me that would also pay the bills. I launched this business in the middle of real life: Covid, perimenopause, parenting pre-teens, supporting a child with an eating disorder, and a blended family of five neurodivergent humans and four needy rescue dogs.

My practice is rooted in radical honesty, deep empathy, fierce vulnerability, and a whole lot of swearing. I believe that you can be both falling apart and rising strong, and I'm here for all of it.

Amy Moreno, MA, LPC · EMDR Certified · IFS-informed

What we work with

Whatever brought you here, someone on this team works with it every week.

  1. ADHD therapy for adults

    Late diagnosis, women and AFAB adults, rejection sensitivity, masking, and the way hormones change everything.

  2. EMDR

    Certified and trained EMDR clinicians, in person or by video. A structured way to help your brain finish processing what got stuck.

  3. IFS and Brainspotting

    For people who want to go underneath the story. Parts work and brain-body approaches for complex trauma.

  4. Neurodivergent and LGBTQIA+ affirming care

    Queer, trans, nonbinary, autistic, ADHD, highly sensitive. You don't have to explain or translate yourself here.

  5. Perinatal and postpartum

    Pre-conception, pregnancy, loss, and the first year with a new baby.

  6. Kids, teens and families

    Kids 7 and up, teens, parents and whole families working through ADHD, autism and big transitions.

  7. Couples counseling

    Communication, betrayal, disconnection and repair, for partners of every configuration, ENM included.

  8. Individual therapy

    Anxiety, depression, grief, burnout, identity and life transitions.

It is okay to feel broken. You're not too much. And you don't have to figure this out alone.

Many of our clients are therapists, caregivers, LGBTQIA+, or late-diagnosed neurodivergent adults who have spent years shape-shifting to be accepted. We see clients in person in Denver, Golden, Boulder and Colorado Springs, and online anywhere in Colorado.
