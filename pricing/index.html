Your website mostly isn't where HIPAA lives. There's no database and no client records on a site I build. The contact form is the exception: a message from someone looking for therapy can count as protected health information once it reaches you. So every build gets one of two setups, and we pick on the first call. One is a short form that asks for a name, a way to reach them and a brief note, and tells people plainly not to share clinical details there. That keeps clinical details out of your inbox as much as a form can. The other runs through a service that signs a Business Associate Agreement, usually your EHR's own contact widget or a secure form service like Hushmail.
