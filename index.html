How this works

Nothing about this should feel complicated.

Four steps. You'll always know which one we're on. No project-management software, no onboarding portal, no homework beyond answering my questions.

i.

A thirty-minute call

Free, and it happens after you've seen the prices below, so it's a conversation, not a pitch. You tell me about your practice and who you want to reach. I tell you honestly whether I'm the right person, and which line on the sheet you actually need.

ii.

Design & build

I design and code your site from scratch, sharing live previews along the way. We iterate on look, voice, and structure together. Usually three to five weeks, start to finish.

iii.

Review & refine

You review the site, request changes, and we fine-tune until everything feels right. I'm responsive and unhurried, this isn't an assembly line.

iv.

Launch & after

Your site goes live. I'm here for post-launch questions for the first 30 days, available on retainer or hourly after that. You're never left on your own.
