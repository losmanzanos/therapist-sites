The written way

Send a note.

Every message lands in my inbox, not a ticket queue. You'll hear back within one business day with a few questions, or with the answer if it's a quick one.

Already have a site? Pick “Free look” below, paste the link, and nobody follows up unless you write back. I’ll go through it the way a new client and Google would, then send a short, plain note on:

  • What comes up when someone searches your name and town
  • Whether Google’s AI and ChatGPT can read the site
  • How fast it loads on an ordinary phone
  • Where your contact form sends what people type
  • The few fixes that would bring in the most calls

The note is free, and it’s yours to keep whether or not we work together.
